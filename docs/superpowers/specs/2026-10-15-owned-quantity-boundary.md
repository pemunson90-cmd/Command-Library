## Exact r1 eligibility precedence (review correction)

Build conflictingPrintingIds from all included demand entries and originally positive inventory lots that assert both Known printing and Known Oracle. A qualified printing asserting more than one distinct qualified Oracle anywhere in this relevant input set is contradictory. Zero lots/excluded board entries contribute neither supply/demand nor mapping assertions. This is conservative snapshot consistency, not catalog-source verification.

For each positive candidate: first hard language mismatch, Known effective-finish versus Known lot-finish mismatch, or exact/pinned Known-printing mismatch → INELIGIBLE. Next, if either known printing is in conflictingPrintingIds → INDETERMINATE (also under exact/pins; cannot bypass it). Otherwise derive identity: exact/pin equal Known printing→ELIGIBLE identity, absent proof→INDETERMINATE; SAME_ORACLE equal Known Oracle→ELIGIBLE identity even with different printings, unequal Known Oracle→INELIGIBLE, missing Oracle proof but equal Known printing→ELIGIBLE identity, otherwise INDETERMINATE. An INELIGIBLE identity stays ineligible even when finish is Unknown. An eligible identity additionally needs proven finish compatibility: effective Absent imposes no constraint; effective Known requires same Known lot finish; Unknown effective/required missing lot finish is INDETERMINATE. A pin supplies Known effective finish and may refine entry Unknown/Absent, but conflicting entry Known finish remains INVALID_INPUT. Thus unrelated unknowns cannot override a hard exclusion.

All originally positive indeterminate candidates mark a partially covered demand incomplete, regardless of allocation to other rows. Full proven cover and IGNORE are complete independently of inventory.complete. This rule deliberately favors honest uncertainty over invented exact missing quantities. Boards/categories/package contributions never multiply entry demand. Canonical policy/output and work limits above bind future packet.
