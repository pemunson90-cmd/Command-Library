## Findings

- **Cancellation and budgets:** real configured connect/read/write timeouts replace the 500 ms workaround. Async enqueue plus cancellable continuation bounds the consumer during blocked DNS; the independent monitor cancels held sockets during blocking reads/writes/TLS and reacts to token, coroutine, close, and overall deadline. Cancellation is rethrown. DNS worker lifetime is explicitly not guaranteed: an uninterruptible resolver may remain occupied after its consumer exits. The fixture verifies no request reaches the wire after resolver release. This is an honest, acceptable consumer guarantee, not proof of resolver termination.
- **Replay:** the pinned RetryAndFollowUpInterceptor really can follow 503 Retry-After:0 and coalesced 421 despite retryOnConnectionFailure=false. Masking those statuses in the network interceptor, restoring them outside follow-up handling, and rejecting a second wire attempt per Call address that behavior. Redirects, cookies, cache and authenticators are disabled. Explicit retry requires NONCHARGED safe GET/HEAD, stops after any emitted chunk, has at most five attempts, and respects the finite job budget. Tests cover actual charged/POST 503 behavior and forced second-proceed rejection. The reported negative replay-control experiment was not rerun by this reviewer.
- **Rate and Retry-After:** shared origin state prevents profile-ID bypass, including 600 ms search and 6 s bulk-index spacing. Positive long numeric/date values retain cooldown and fail closed beyond the available retry/request budget even with honorRetryAfter=false. Numeric overflow saturates; malformed values fail without replay. Acquisition must retain its sequential crawl scheduling and use the reviewed source routes; this adapter is not evidence of a completed acquisition scheduler.
- **Bounds and metadata:** stream chunks are at most 64 KiB, offsets are contiguous, send has a separate 8 MiB default ceiling, and truncation/overflow never emits final success. Raw gzip, Range and validators remain visible. Header validation covers redirects/errors as well as success, counts repeated entries, and measures serialized UTF-8 sizes. Pinned engine source also contains HTTP/2 HPACK header-byte limit checks, supplementing its HTTP/1 parser limit. Durable resume/content validation remains the caller's job.
- **Security and lifecycle:** HTTPS and exact allowed origins are checked before calls and redirects; userinfo/fragments are rejected; cross-origin hops retain only Accept, Accept-Encoding and User-Agent. Explicit and global cookie/authentication credentials cannot be inherited through the engine. Secret references fail KEY_MISSING; transport diagnostics contain no URL, body or credential text. Response ownership, cancellation cleanup, close-abort and new-call rejection are coherent.
