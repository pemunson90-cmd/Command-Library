# Authentic fixture publication policy

K3 local-first fixture policy. This governs source bytes published in this public repository; it does not add approval requirements for routine synthetic tests or local implementation.

Synthetic fixtures must be labeled and may verify parser behavior, bounds and failure recovery. They cannot establish authentic source grammar, complete coverage, current upstream counts or measured corpus performance.

Do not commit complete third-party feeds or rules texts with unresolved redistribution rights. Before publishing genuine copied material, record the exact source URL/access date, selected bytes/hash, applicable license or permission, notices, intended distribution surface and independent source-review disposition. A small excerpt, public download, redaction or a checksum is not itself permission. There is no universal safe excerpt byte threshold.

Genuine personal-use validation may use ignored local files under the existing D-13/D-14 acquisition scope. Store public evidence as source URL, claimed version/date (or unknown), exact bytes/hash, language/counts, parser revision and observed results. Do not put source payloads into public logs, CI artifacts, screenshots, generated databases or APKs without source-specific distribution review. A hash establishes identity, not rights or completeness.

Permitted genuine-derived fixtures need a reproducible derivation recipe and attribution beside the fixture; label modifications and never describe sanitized data as raw upstream bytes. Existing committed fixtures remain retained historical evidence; this policy does not retroactively claim their permission has been reviewed.

Owner: K3 orchestrator for E01/E02 evidence; later transfer owner for E04 authentic vendor round trips. Evidence: docs/verification/k3-gap-execution/SOURCE-EVIDENCE.md. Independent policy/source review is recorded alongside that evidence. The policy does not authorize automatic downloads, public corpus bundling or non-English acquisition.
